# MiniProject
## INDIE DIETYY
- The Indian diet planner leverages AI to create personalized meal and nutrition recommendations tailored to Indian cuisine, user preferences, and local eating habits, overcoming the challenge of accurately quantifying diverse regional meals like thalis and home-cooked recipes.
- Most other AI diet planners focus primarily on Western foods and use generic databases, offering features like calorie tracking, automated grocery lists, and recipe generation based on user health goals but often lack support for Indian dishes or cultural context.
- Unlike global planners, Indie Dietyy's solution enables multi-workout plans(weight gain, weight loss, muscle toning) ,recognizes real Indian foods that are locally available, progress tracking and affordable for the users where it aligns with the diet plans.


## About
Indie Dietyy is a web-based personalized diet planning system developed using Python and Streamlit. It is designed to provide affordable Indian diet recommendations based on the user’s location, age, weight, height, allergies, and health requirements. Many existing diet applications suggest western foods or costly supplements that are not suitable for everyday Indian users. Indie Dietyy addresses this issue by focusing on regional and budget-friendly Indian foods that users can easily access in their locality. The system calculates BMI and calorie requirements, filters foods based on allergies, and generates a customized meal plan using available and low-cost Indian options. It specifically benefits fitness enthusiasts, older adults, and individuals who require diet planning based on affordability and cultural preferences. Through its regional food database and health filtering logic, Indie Dietyy aims to make nutrition practical, accessible, and suitable for everyday Indian lifestyles.

## Features
- Generates customized Indian diet suggestions based on user inputs.
- Allows users to enter age, weight, height, and dietary goals.
- Provides location-based food recommendations through country and state selection.
- Filters out restricted food items using an allergy input system.
- Focuses on affordable and regionally available Indian foods instead of calorie calculations.
- Offers a simple Streamlit-based interface for quick diet plan generation.

## Results and Impact
The Indie Dietyy system generates personalized meal plans based on individual body metrics and regional food preferences, producing accurate calorie estimates and balanced nutrient distributions. The project effectively proves that customized diets are more practical and sustainable than generic diet charts, especially when aligned with Indian cuisine. Users receive culturally relevant meal suggestions that promote healthy eating without drastically changing food habits. The impact of this system lies in encouraging long-term lifestyle improvement, increasing awareness of nutrition, and supporting health goals such as weight control and fitness. Overall, the project demonstrates how technology can simplify diet planning and make personalized nutrition accessible to everyone.
